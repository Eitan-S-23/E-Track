## Failure Decisions

Before command publication, any image/BCB mismatch prohibits the transaction.
After an uncertain command, inspect the mailbox and EEPROM snapshots without
reissuing it. A failed/unknown flash or reset is reconciled by passive inspection,
not replay. After a committed rollback, the original Boot must finish normally;
do not hurry it with another reset. Escalate only a real unsafe state, unresolved
device outcome or required physical action. Preserve all failed receipts.
